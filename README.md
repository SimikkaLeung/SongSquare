Song Square is an online platform created by and for music lovers, where users can share and review songs publicly and privately.
To ensure Song Square is music-oriented, each post of regular users requires at least a link to a supported music website or application, such as Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music, and iTunes.
Users can see public posts as well as content published by accounts they follow in their feed.
Users can see their own content on their walls.
Statistics will be collected and may be shared with third parties including record labels, music streaming platforms, and media outlets.
More features may follow.
